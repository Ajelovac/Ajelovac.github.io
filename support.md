# Kyouka Support

**Kyouka** shows you one kanji a day via a Home Screen or Lock Screen widget, with a Marks list,
Dictionary, and Settings to configure what each widget shows.

## Contact

Questions, bug reports, or feature requests: email <kyouka@gmail.com>.

## Common questions

**Why does my widget show different content than the app?**
Open the app at least once after installing — the widget reads from a shared container the app sets up on
first launch, and needs it signed in to the same App Group. If you change a widget's content fields in
Settings and an already-placed widget doesn't update, try removing and re-adding it.

**Why does Dictionary search or the main screen only show a few kanji?**
Kyouka's free tier includes the seven weekday kanji with full functionality (marks, notes, widgets). The
paid unlock (monthly subscription or one-time lifetime purchase) reaches the full kanji pool. Both tiers
grant identical app functionality — only how much of the dictionary is reachable differs.

**Will my marks and notes sync across my devices?**
Yes, automatically, as long as you're signed in to iCloud on each device — Kyouka uses your own private
iCloud storage for this, the same way Apple's own Notes app does. No separate account or sign-in inside
Kyouka is needed.

**Does Kyouka track me or show ads?**
No. See the [Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.html) for the full detail — there is no analytics, no
third-party SDK, and no advertising anywhere in the app.
